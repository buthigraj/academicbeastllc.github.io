Why Science Bee Coach

Practice the way
the Science Bee actually works

🐝

Pyramidal Buzzer

Practice like you compete. Questions are read aloud sentence by sentence using natural neural text-to-speech. Buzz in early on the first clue for maximum points, or wait for more context. The scoring mirrors the real competition: earlier buzzes earn more points.

📝

ORQE Simulator

For Elementary / Middle School: a timed 50-question multiple-choice exam drawn from real ORQE question banks across multiple years. Covers all major science topics with a 20-minute timer, just like the real qualifying exam.

🎓

NQE Simulator

For High School / JV / Varsity: practice the National Qualifying Exam format with 50 real NQE questions per session, randomly sampled from a bank of 448 questions spanning three competition years. Pause the timer, jump between questions, and review your answers before submitting.

📚

Flashcards

Study by topic — Chemistry, Cell Biology, Astronomy, Earth Science, Physics, Zoology, Mathematics, Disease & Medicine, and more. Cards are drawn from the real question archive and organized by your weak areas so you spend time where it counts most.

⚡

Power Drill

Rapid-fire multiple choice questions filtered by topic, difficulty, and school level. Perfect for short study sessions between classes.

🔥

Daily Challenge

A new set of questions every day combining a pyramidal tossup with a multiple choice round. Build a daily streak and track your improvement over time.
